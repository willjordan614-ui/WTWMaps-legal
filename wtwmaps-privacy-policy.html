WTWMaps Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how WTWMaps collects, uses, and protects information when you use the app.

1. Introduction

WTWMaps (“we,” “our,” or “us”) is a location-based event discovery app that helps users find and share local events. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about how information is collected, used, and shared.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Location Data

We may collect location data only when you grant permission in order to:

  • Display nearby events
  • Improve map accuracy
  • Enable optional heatmap features

Safeguards: Location is used only while the app is in use. We do not track location in the background. Heatmap data is intended to be aggregated and anonymized, and heatmaps appear only when multiple users are present.

2.2 User-Generated Content

When you contribute content, we may collect:

  • Event details such as title, date, time, and location
  • Uploaded images such as event flyers
  • Descriptions or edits you provide

This content may be visible within the app.

2.3 Camera and Photo Library Access

With your permission, WTWMaps may access your camera or photo library so you can take or upload flyer images. We only access media when you choose to use those features.

2.4 AI-Processed Content

When you upload a flyer, the image may be processed by AI services to extract event details such as the event name, date, time, location, and cost. Images are processed only to support event submission and extraction workflows.

2.5 Device and Usage Data

We may collect limited technical information such as device type, operating system version, and app usage data for debugging, performance, and reliability.

3. How We Use Information

  • Provide core app functionality
  • Display relevant local events
  • Process flyer uploads into structured event listings
  • Improve app performance, reliability, and security
  • Prevent abuse or misuse of the platform

4. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area, we may process data under the following bases:

  • Consent for location, camera, and photo permissions
  • Legitimate interests for platform functionality, safety, and improvements
  • User-provided content when you submit event information or flyer images

5. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell personal data.

We may share limited data with service providers that help operate the app, such as:

  • Backend and database providers, including Supabase
  • AI processing providers, including OpenAI, for flyer text extraction
  • Infrastructure or hosting providers that support app performance and delivery

These providers process data on our behalf and are not authorized to use it for unrelated purposes.

6. Data Retention

We retain data only as long as reasonably necessary to provide the app and maintain relevant event listings.

  • Event data may be stored while active or relevant
  • Uploaded flyer content may be stored to maintain listings and moderation workflows
  • Technical logs may be kept for a limited period for debugging and security

7. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Request data portability where applicable

8. California Privacy Rights (CCPA/CPRA)

If you are a California resident, you may have rights to know what categories of personal information are collected, request deletion of personal information, and request correction of certain information. WTWMaps does not sell personal information.

9. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect data. However, no system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Children's Privacy

WTWMaps is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

11. Your Choices

  • You can disable location permissions in your device settings
  • You can deny camera or photo access
  • You can choose not to upload content
  • You can request deletion of your data by contacting us

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will post the updated version within the app or on the website where this policy is hosted.
